how I test

A clear, consistent approach, not just clicking around.

01

Explore

Play with intent, pushing mechanics, edge cases, and player choices to see where a game holds up and where it doesn't.

02

Document

Record issues clearly and consistently, so bugs are easy for a team to reproduce, prioritise, and fix.

03

Verify

Re-test fixes and check nothing else broke in the process, closing the loop, not just flagging problems.

04

Communicate

Feed findings back in a way that's useful to designers and developers, always with the player's experience in mind.
